The problems

Three B2B software vendor problems, and why they're connected.

01

Your channel partners don't sell your ISV product the way you'd sell it

Partners default to selling what they know. Your ISV product becomes a line item, not a story. We build positioning and channel enablement assets your partners can actually use, in their voice, with their customers, to their priorities. That's what puts your software into real B2B sales conversations.

02

End-customers don't know which software category you're in

If buyers can't place you in a category, they can't evaluate you, and they can't buy you. We define your category positioning, clarify who you're for, and build messaging that makes the buying decision easier at every stage of the funnel.

03

Your ISV roadmap moves quarterly. Your channel messaging doesn't.

Co-Pilot today, Azure last quarter, cybersecurity before that. Your partner enablement assets, messaging, and campaign briefs need to keep pace with your ISV priorities. Blacfox rebuilds your partner-facing message stack quarterly, so your priorities show up in the channel within weeks, not at the next QBR.

ISV questions

How Blacfox works with ISVs.

What is channel enablement for ISVs?

Channel enablement for ISVs is the process of equipping partner resellers and implementers with the positioning, messaging, sales assets, and training they need to sell an ISV's product effectively. Effective ISV channel enablement means partners can explain what the software does, who it's for, why it's different from competitors, and how to handle common objections, without the ISV being in the room.

How does Blacfox help ISVs generate demand through their partner channel?

Blacfox works with ISVs in two motions simultaneously: demand generation at the end-customer level (so buyers are asking for your product by name) and channel enablement at the partner level (so partners are actively selling it and positioned correctly). The two motions reinforce each other, end-customer pull makes your product easier for partners to sell, and partner-led positioning makes campaigns more credible to end-buyers.
