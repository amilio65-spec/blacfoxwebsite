The problems

Three distributor problems, and why they're connected.

01

Your reseller network defaults to the vendor lines they already know

A reseller with ten vendor lines on their price list will keep pitching the two they understand. Your other lines sit there, fully stocked, quietly unsold. We build positioning and enablement assets for each vendor line you carry, written so a reseller's sales rep can explain it, without you in the room.

02

Co-op and MDF funding across multiple vendors is a full-time reconciliation job

Every vendor programme has its own eligibility rules, its own proof-of-execution standard, and its own deadlines, and they don't line up. Most distributors either under-claim across the portfolio or burn internal hours reconciling paperwork instead of running campaigns. We manage multi-vendor funded campaigns end-to-end, across your whole vendor portfolio, not just one line.

03

Vendors measure you on sell-through. Your resellers measure success differently.

Vendors want revenue moving through the channel. Resellers want qualified conversations they can close. Those are two different scoreboards, and most distributor marketing only reports on one. We measure on pipeline ACV at the reseller level, the number that proves the campaign worked to both audiences at once.

Distributor questions

How Blacfox works with distributors.

What is channel enablement for distributors?

Channel enablement for distributors means equipping the resellers in your network with the positioning, messaging, and sales assets to sell a vendor's product confidently, for every vendor line you carry, not just the ones they already know well. Done well, a reseller's rep can explain what a product does, who it's for, and why it beats the alternative, without your team or the vendor's team in the room.

How does Blacfox coordinate co-op/MDF funding across multiple vendors for one distributor?

We track each vendor's programme separately, its eligibility rules, proof requirements, and deadlines, but plan the campaign calendar for your whole portfolio as one system. That way no single vendor's funding cycle gets deprioritised because another vendor's deadline landed in the same quarter.
