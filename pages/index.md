<section class="bg-white">

    <p class="section-tag reveal">Common questions</p>
    <h2 class="section-h reveal">What people <em>ask us</em> before they call.</h2>

    <div class="faq-list stagger">
      <div class="faq-item">
        <p class="faq-q">What is pipeline ACV and how does Blacfox measure it?</p>
        <p class="faq-a">Pipeline ACV (Annual Contract Value) is the total annualised value of qualified sales opportunities created by a marketing campaign.
