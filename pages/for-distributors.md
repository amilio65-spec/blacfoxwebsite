      <div class="dark-card" data-problem="enablement">

        <p class="card-title">Your reseller network defaults to the vendor lines they already know</p>
        <p class="card-body">A reseller with ten vendor lines on their price list will keep pitching the two they understand. Your other lines sit there, fully stocked, quietly unsold.       </div>
      <div class="dark-card" data-problem="funding">
        <p class="card-num">02</p>
        <p class="card-title">Co-op and MDF funding across multiple vendors is a full-time reconciliation job</p>
        <p class="card-body">Every vendor programme has its own eligibility rules, its own proof-of-execution standard, and its own deadlines, and they don't line up. Most distributors either under-claim across the portfolio or burn internal hours reconciling paperwork instead of running campaigns. We manage multi-vendor funded campaigns end-to-end, across your whole vendor portfolio, not just one line.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="dark-card" data-problem="both">
        <p class="card-num">03</p>
        <p class="card-title">Vendors measure you on sell-through. Your resellers measure success differently.</p>
        <p class="card-body">Vendors want revenue moving through the channel. Resellers want qualified conversations they can close. Those are two different scoreboards, and most distributor marketing only reports on one. We measure on pipeline ACV at the reseller level, the number that proves the campaign worked to both audiences at once.</p>
