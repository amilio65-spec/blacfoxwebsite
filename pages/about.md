<!-- S04 FOUR OPINIONS -->
<section class="bg-white">
  <div class="section-inner">
    <p class="section-tag reveal">How we think</p>
    <h2 class="section-h reveal">Four <em>opinions</em> that shape every B2B tech engagement.</h2>

    <div class="card-grid-2 stagger" style="margin-top:48px;">
      <div class="dark-card">
        <p class="card-num">01</p>
        <p class="card-title">Qualified meetings are the outcome. Leads aren't.</p>
        <p class="card-body">Every B2B marketing agency claim is built on lead volume. Leads don't pay invoices. We measure what marketing actually delivered to the sales team, qualified meetings with decision-makers who are ready to talk, and stay involved until that handoff works.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="dark-card">
        <p class="card-num">02</p>
        <p class="card-title">Research before everything.</p>
        <p class="card-body">We don't build a PMF from assumptions. We talk to real buyers first, every time. Their language, their trade-offs, their alternatives, their fears. The research is the most expensive step and the most important one.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="dark-card">
        <p class="card-num">03</p>
        <p class="card-title">Positioning is the work. Campaigns are the delivery.</p>
        <p class="card-body">Most agencies skip straight to campaigns. We don't. Without positioning, campaigns spend budget re-educating buyers about who you are. With it, every rand works harder from day one.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="dark-card">
        <p class="card-num">04</p>
        <p class="card-title">Stay in the room past the lead.</p>
        <p class="card-body">The hand-off from marketing to sales is where most B2B programmes fail. We stay involved (SDR follow-up, meeting support, sales collateral) until the pipeline moves. Not until the campaign ends.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
